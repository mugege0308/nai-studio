# Japanese supplement attribution

`translations-ja.json` is a NAI Studio-maintained supplement. It is separate
from the generated files sourced from `PYU224/tagdb-updater` and is never
overwritten by the weekly upstream update.

The entries are conservative Japanese translations for general and meta tags
that can be translated from clear English wording. Proper names, works,
artists, ambiguous phrases, and sensitive terms are intentionally left in
English. The application uses this order when displaying a result:

1. Japanese text supplied by the upstream dictionary
2. This supplement
3. The original English tag

The supplement is generated by `scripts/build_japanese_supplement.py`. It is
not a blanket translation of upstream records and does not change upstream
attribution or licensing terms.
